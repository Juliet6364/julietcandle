Candle care

Get the most from every burn

2–3 hr

The first burn

Let the wax melt right to the edge the first time. This stops the candle tunnelling down the middle.

5 mm

Trim the wick

Trim before every light for a calm flame and less soot on the glass.

4 hr max

Rest between burns

Blow out after four hours and let it cool fully before lighting again.

on a plate

Sculpted pieces

Swirls and rose hearts have no container. Always burn them on a heat-safe plate, away from drafts.

About

Handcrafted with heart.

Flicker n Flame is Juliet's small candle studio. Each candle is poured in soy wax, unmoulded and finished by hand, so no two are exactly alike. Petals, pebbles and pearls are all wax, added one at a time.
