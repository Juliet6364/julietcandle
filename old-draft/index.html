How a candle gets made

  1. Melt and scent

    Soy wax is melted slowly, then blended with fragrance oil.

  2. Pour

    The wax is poured by hand around a centred cotton wick.

  3. Cure

    Candles rest for days so the scent settles and burns evenly.

  4. Trim and pack

    Each wick is trimmed to size before the candle is packed.
